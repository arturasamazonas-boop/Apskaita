# Testing

All tests run against a real PostgreSQL 16 database. They drop and recreate the dedicated test database `apskaita_test`; never point them at real data. They use real system tools (poppler, Tesseract 5 `lit+eng`, ImageMagick, xmllint) and the committed fixtures in `fixtures/` (fictional companies, regenerated by `npm run fixtures`).

## Chart of accounts, products and stock, payroll (`10-chart-products-payroll`)

| Area | What the test checks |
|---|---|
| Chart | Tree imported from the workbook. Posting roles. Pickers list leaf accounts only. Posting to a group is rejected by the API and by a raw insert (DB trigger). A sub-account turns an unused leaf into a group. A used account cannot be split. Sub-account codes must start with the parent code. |
| Upgrade | A database migrated to 008 with postings on old starter accounts is upgraded. Used accounts are deactivated and keep their postings; unused ones are removed. Bank accounts are repointed. |
| Products | All card fields are saved. SKU is unique. A group account is refused. Stock comes from opening balance, write-off (reason required), and an approved purchase and sale linked to the product. Also checked: totals, weighted average cost, balance on a date, the movement list with running balance, immutability of movements, low-stock filter, partial update, and services without stock. |
| Payroll calculation | 2026 NPD formula, GPM, VSD/PSD, employer Sodra. Net pay at 2000 € and at MMA. NPD phase-out and cap. Fixed NPD. Pro-rata days, extra pension and the fixed-term employer rate. Hourly pay. The working-day norm with holidays. |
| Payroll sheet | Employees. A masked personal code for read-only users. Sheet creation with the norm and pro-rata start. Editing bonus and advance. One sheet per month. Read-only cannot approve. Approval posts the expected amounts to 6304/6203, 4480, 4481 and 4482. Double approval posts once. An approved sheet is read-only. The journal source link. Cancel by reversal, re-approval with a new entry, and no deletion after approval. |

## Other checks

- **Malicious instructions** inside an invoice stay inert text (`01`, last test); LLM output schema and allowlist filtering.
- **Rounding:** exact decimals, half-up, per-line VAT tolerance, allocation sums (`00-unit`).
- **Concurrent numbering:** 8 parallel approvals produce gap-free unique numbers (`04`).
- **Provider failure and retry:** a corrupt PDF is retried 5 times, then marked failed with an actionable message, then manually retried; a transient failure is retried successfully; idempotent enqueue (`04`).
- **Re-extraction** of a posted document leaves it unchanged; the correction posts only the delta (`04`).
- **Extraction fixtures** cover native PDF, DOCX tables, a scanned PDF with a smudged amount, a skewed phone photo (JPG), a 90°-rotated scan (PNG, OSD), a 2-page 42-line invoice, two invoices in one file, a proforma, a credit note, a sales invoice, and a legacy `.doc` (rejected with an explanation).
- **Integrations:** Saleor via a mock GraphQL/JWKS server with real JWS signatures; OpenCart via the real extension under `php -S`; webhook duplicates, out-of-order delivery, 429 retry and resume, demo labelling (`05`).
- **Database and file backup** → restore → verification → app restart on the restored copy (`07`).
- **Container:** the Docker image was built and run with docker compose (PostgreSQL 16 + app). Admin creation, login, upload and OCR of the scanned invoice inside the container, and the i.SAF XSD check were all exercised manually with a script.

## Not verified

- No live Saleor or OpenCart store was used; integration tests are fixture/mock tests. See the live checklist in [INTEGRATIONS.md](INTEGRATIONS.md).
- The vendored i.SAF XSD was not compared with the file on vmi.lt (blocked); see [ISAF.md](ISAF.md).
- The optional Anthropic LLM provider was not called (no credentials). Only its output validation is tested.
- No ClamAV scanning (not installed).
- No restore onto a different host; no load testing with concurrent users.
- Accessibility was checked only through semantic markup, keyboard focus styles and the browser test's role-based selectors. No screen-reader audit was done.

## Company details (`11-company-details`)

The tests cover:

- Parsing rekvizitai.lt page text where the label and value are on separate lines. The page header and footer are included as noise.
- Same-line labels, a company that is not a VAT payer, an MB, e-mail and phone extraction, name normalisation (`Energitech, UAB` → `UAB „Energitech“`), and the bookmarklet URL.
- The VIES check against a local stub: valid with name, invalid, service down (502) and a malformed code (400).
- Counterparty extra fields, refusal of a duplicate company code, and the new company setting fields, including that fields not sent are kept.

The real VIES service was called once manually from this environment and returned a valid result with the name.

The rekvizitai.lt page layout itself could not be fetched here because of Cloudflare. The parser therefore relies on the usual labels plus format checks (9-digit code, `LT` + 9/12-digit VAT code, LT IBAN). Check it on a real page.

## Financial statements (`12-statements`)

The tests post sample entries for 2025–2026 and check:

- the full balance sheet item values, including the year result, the previous-years result and the previous-year column;
- that total assets equal total equity and liabilities;
- the abbreviated and very small balance sheets, with whole-euro rounding;
- that the full and short P&L give the same net result;
- for the short P&L, that bought stock plus the change in stock equals the cost of goods sold;
- hand-entered previous-period amounts: totals cannot be set directly, read-only users are refused, values can be cleared;
- the PDF download.

The PDF layout was also checked visually by rendering pages to images.

## Cash flow, equity, FR0600, debt act (`13-cashflow-equity`)

The sample year 2026 includes:

- customer and supplier payments, a cash sale and cost of sales;
- salaries accrued and paid, interest;
- a bank loan received and partly repaid;
- dividends declared and paid, a legal reserve;
- equipment bought and sold at a gain.

The tests check:

- the indirect method line by line: net result, working-capital changes, eliminations, investing, financing, and V = VII − VI;
- the direct method by counter-account, giving the same net change;
- the statement of changes in equity over two years, with movements reconciling and row 30 equal to equity in the balance sheet, and its PDF;
- FR0600 fields from approved invoices at 21 % and 12 % with deductible purchase VAT;
- the debt reconciliation act and its PDF.

## FR0600 as in EDS (`14-eds-fr0600`)

- The layout matches the VMI template: form ID, 45 fields, cell counts, read-only 36, radio group items.
- Values are in whole euros (8.40 → 8) and empty fields stay empty. Pirminė and Patikslinta are tested. Right-aligned cell placement is checked, and dates skip the dashes.
- Edits: 36 is recalculated, read-only fields are ignored, and invalid amounts, percentages and unknown fields are refused.
- The `.ffdata` download has the EDS structure and values including edits. The PDF download is also checked.

The PDF and the web form were also checked visually: characters fall into the form's boxes.

## RC set, fixed assets, year-end, SEPA, GPM313, regressions (`15`–`19`)

- **`15-rc-package`:**
  - Size category and forms.
  - Cross-checks between the forms.
  - Year-end closing into 341 keeps the P&L equal to the balance sheet result; after the transfer to 342, 341 counts as prior-year.
  - The set PDF (pages) and CSV/XLSX.
- **`16-fixed-assets`:**
  - Monthly amount and last-month remainder, plus the opening accumulated depreciation.
  - Runs must go in order and cannot repeat a month; the posted entry is checked.
  - Financial fields are frozen after posting.
  - Only the latest run can be cancelled.
  - Disposal needs the disposal month posted, and its entry is checked.
  - The register reconciles with the ledger.
- **`17-regressions`:**
  - A credit note takes a pro-rata share of the line discount.
  - A negative line gets negative VAT.
  - FR0600 field 25 holds all purchase VAT and field 35 the deductible VAT.
  - An hourly rate with 4 decimals.
  - 32 % GPM and the VSD ceiling above 60 VDU.
  - Bad input returns 400 or 404, never 500.
- **`18-sepa-payments`:**
  - Transliteration.
  - Amount and IBAN checks.
  - The invoice and payroll files validate against the ISO 20022 XSD.
- **`19-eds-gpm313`:**
  - The template layout.
  - The split at the 15th by payment date.
  - Decimal cells.
  - Edits, FFData and PDF.

## PLN204, SAM, cash orders (`20`–`22`)

- **`20-eds-pln204`:**
  - Template layouts, and rates by code and year.
  - The loss branch.
  - Fields 18 and 19 from the ledger, with profit tax excluded.
  - Non-deductible rules → PLN204S rows → 20.
  - The chain to 64 at the reduced rate.
  - Edits: standard rate, the 70 % loss limit, validation, and tax depreciation reducing 37.
  - The multi-page FFData with its annex group, a 3-page PDF, and editing the rules.
- **`21-sodra-sam`:**
  - Rows from the payroll sheet, the combined rate for indefinite and fixed-term contracts with pension accumulation, and people who left being excluded.
  - Pages of 6 persons.
  - FFData groups, pages and field counts as in the official sample, and the PDF.
- **`22-cash-orders`:**
  - Amounts in words.
  - KPO settling a sales invoice, KIO paying a supplier, an accountable person, and a transfer to the bank.
  - Cash cannot go negative, and an invoice cannot be overpaid.
  - The cash book by day.
  - Cancellation makes the invoice unpaid again; cancelling a KPO that would leave cash negative is refused.
  - The PDF.
